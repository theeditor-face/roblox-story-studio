# dont_sneeze - "In this world, sneezing makes you smaller. The bigger the sneeze, the tinier you get."
I sneezed during Picture Day, and I shrank down to the size of a thumbtack! Then the kid next to me sneezed too, and he disappeared into his own shoe!
You see, in this world, sneezing makes you smaller. The bigger the sneeze, the tinier you get.
That’s why hospitals ban pepper from the cafeteria. Astronauts quarantine away from dust for a full week before liftoff. And my uncle sleeps with the vacuum unplugged, just in case it tips over.
But I have a problem. I’m allergic to pollen, dust, cats, and apparently feelings. And yesterday, the Picture Day photographer brought a bouquet of flowers for the “spring theme.”
One sniff and — ACHOO! I shrank past my chair, past the floor tiles, all the way down to the size of a raisin.
My friend Mia looked down, gasped, and sneezed from the shock. She popped down right beside me, now the size of a grape.
Then the camera flash went off. Pollen puffed into the air. The whole class breathed it in at once. Thirty kids sneezed in one giant chorus and shrank down like popped balloons. Backpacks and the tripod towered over us like skyscrapers.
The sneezing didn’t stop at the classroom door. It drifted into the hallway, into the office, into the vents of the entire town. Dogs barked. Car horns blared. Then — ACHOO, a thousand times at once. The whole town shrank together. Buildings turned into mountains. Puddles turned into oceans.
There was only one person who never sneezed. Not once, not ever. Old Man Higgins. Allergic to nothing, immune to everything. While the whole town shrank around him, he stayed exactly his normal size — now a giant looming over all of us.
Then I noticed something. Higgins was the only one left tall enough to reach anything.
I climbed up his shoelace and cupped my hands. “HIGGINS! WE NEED A LIFT!”
He scooped up the whole shrunken town in one hand — cars, dogs, the mayor, all of us — and carried everyone straight to the nurse’s office for the biggest allergy shot in history.
Everyone grew back to normal size. The whole town cheered for Higgins. And for the first time ever, he smiled so wide his nose twitched.
Then he sneezed. Oh no. The biggest sneeze in history. Somebody hold onto something!
