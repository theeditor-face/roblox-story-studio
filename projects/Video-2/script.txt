# truth_disappear - "In this world, telling the truth makes you disappear. The bigger the truth, the longer you're gone."
I told my mom I didn’t eat the last cookie, and I vanished. Completely. Mid-sentence. For eleven seconds.
You see, in this world, telling the truth makes you disappear. The bigger the truth, the longer you’re gone.
That’s why couples therapists work entirely by phone. Lie detector tests got replaced with just... waiting in a room. And my dad hasn’t answered “does this make me look old” honestly in six years.
But today, I got called as a witness. In an actual courtroom. Under oath.
The judge looked down at me. “Did you or did you not see who broke the trophy case?”
I saw Leo do it.
My hand was already on the Bible. My mouth opened. I tried to say “no.” I really did.
“...Leo.”
Gone.
Not just me — the second the truth left my mouth, the whole courtroom flickered. Leo shot up from the gallery, “WAIT, THAT’S NOT—” and he vanished too, admitting it by accident.
The judge slammed her gavel. “ORDER! Both witnesses are invisible, we cannot have an invisible tr—”
She vanished mid-sentence.
Then the bailiff. Then the court reporter. Then all twelve people in the jury box, one by one, like dominoes, each one whispering something true under their breath without meaning to.
The room was almost empty. Just floating microphones and an empty judge’s robe, still standing upright.
Then I heard Leo’s voice, right next to me, panicked: “Wait... if everyone in here is honest right now... who’s actually driving the courthouse elevator outside?”
